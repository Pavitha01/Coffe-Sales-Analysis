# Coffee Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes coffee shop sales data using MySQL and Power BI. The goal is to understand sales performance, order activity, product performance, and sales trends.

## Tools Used
- **MySQL:** Data preparation and SQL-based analysis.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and visual reporting.
- **GitHub:** Project documentation and version control.

## Business Questions

This project analyzes coffee shop sales data to help the business understand its operational performance, customer behaviors, and revenue drivers. The analysis is structured to answer the following key business questions and KPI requirements:

### 1. Total Sales Analysis
*   *Calculate the total sales for each respective month.*
    *   Objective: Identify baseline monthly revenue figures across the entire data timeline.
*   *Determine the month-on-month increase or decrease in sales.*
    *   Objective: Measure short-term revenue growth rate and directional trends.
*   *Calculate the difference in sales between the selected month and the previous month.*
    *   Objective: Quantify the absolute currency value fluctuations from one month to the next.

### 2. Total Orders Analysis
*   *Calculate the total number of orders for each respective month.*
    *   Objective: Track transactional volume variations across different operational periods.
*   *Determine the month-on-month increase or decrease in the number of orders.*
    *   Objective: Monitor whether client transaction volume is growing or shrinking over time.
*   *Calculate the difference in the number of orders between the selected month and the previous month.*
    *   Objective: Measure the exact net change in order tickets processed between consecutive months.

### 3. Total Quantity Sold Analysis
*   *Calculate the total quantity sold for each respective month.*
    *   Objective: Understand unit-level movement patterns across all beverage and food categories.
*   *Determine the month-on-month increase or decrease in the total quantity sold.*
    *   Objective: Evaluate shifting item-volume velocities to track inventory demand behaviors.
*   *Calculate the difference in the total quantity sold between the selected month and the previous month.*
    *   Objective: Pinpoint the exact volume differences in units shifted from the immediate prior month.
